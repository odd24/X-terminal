- 冻结的原文以 `git ls-tree -r 8ad5cc2 -- contracts/mcp crates/session-protocol/src apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/main.rs apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/session_windows.rs apps/desktop/src/main.tsx` 复取。对应 blob 清单见 `contract-source-blobs.txt`。R4–R7 前补各自的动态拒绝、错误和回执样本。
